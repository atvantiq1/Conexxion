Our story

A decade of delivery, not decks.

Conexxion Tech India was incorporated in December 2015 in Bangalore, with a simple thesis: India's infrastructure boom — telecom, data centers, cloud and the talent powering all three — needed firms that could execute, not just consult. Firms that would show up at a tower site at 6 AM, document every cable run, and sign their name to the acceptance certificate.

We started small, with a focused IT staffing practice serving system integrators in South India. Within three years, our staffing relationships had grown into delivery relationships — clients began asking us to bring in not just engineers, but full project teams. By 2019, we had formal practice areas in telecom and data center delivery. Cloud infrastructure followed in 2021, as our enterprise clients began their serious migrations.

Today, headquartered in Mysore with delivery presence across 28 states, we work with Tier-1 operators, system integrators, hyperscaler partners and enterprises — across talent, towers, data and cloud. We've done it without venture capital, without acquisitions, and without a single missed payroll. Promoter-led, profitable, deliberately built.

We're not the loudest firm in our category. We're the one your peers quietly recommend after a project that actually went well.

Our mission

What we're here to do.

We exist to help India's enterprises and operators build the infrastructure their customers depend on every second — with the discipline of seasoned engineers, the agility of an owner-run firm, and the boring rigour that turns 99.5% uptime into 99.98%.
What we stand for

Six values that actually shape how we work.

01

Delivery is the deliverable

We measure ourselves on whether the project closed on time, on scope and on budget — not on how good the kickoff deck was.

02

Documentation is non-optional

Every site, server, cable and configuration is documented. Year three is when you find out whether year one was done right.

03

Honest scoping

We won't oversell timelines or capabilities to win business. Painful in the sales cycle, profitable in the renewal cycle.

04

Engineer-led decisions

Our practice leads are practitioners, not pure account managers. Hard technical calls are made by people who can defend them.

05

Field discipline

Safety, quality audits, peer review and chain-of-custody on every site. The boring practices that prevent expensive incidents.

06

Long relationships

We're built for repeat business — most of our clients are in their third engagement. We protect long-term reputation over short-term margin.

Milestones

From a three-person staffing desk to a four-practice firm.

2015 · DECEMBER

Conexxion Tech India incorporated

Registered with the Bangalore ROC as a private limited company. Authorized capital of ₹50 lakh. First office: a single room in Mysore.

2016

First enterprise staffing engagement

Began with a focused practice in IT staffing for system integrators across South India — placements primarily in BFSI and manufacturing infrastructure roles.

2018

Telecom services launched

Formalized the telecom practice — site engineering, RAN integration and FTTx rollouts — with the first 50-site greenfield engagement for a regional ISP.

2020

First data center build engagement

Delivered our first turnkey white-space build — 80 racks across two halls — for an enterprise client in Bangalore. Crossed 100 cumulative engineers placed.

2021

Cloud infrastructure practice formed

Launched the cloud practice with AWS and Azure specialists, in response to enterprise migration demand. First migration engagement: 240 workloads from on-prem to AWS.

2023

Pan-India delivery footprint

Crossed 1,000 cumulative sites deployed and 200 cumulative engineers placed. Formalized 14-city delivery hubs and mobilization SLAs.

2025

A decade in delivery

Crossed 1,200 sites, 200+ engineers placed, 15+ data center projects and 40+ cloud engagements. Signed our 50th repeat-business contract.

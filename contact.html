Before you write

A few common questions, quickly answered.

How quickly will I get a response?

Within one business day for general enquiries, often the same day for project requests received before 4 PM IST.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes — we serve global system integrators and hyperscalers with India-based delivery, and selectively take on cross-border engagements.

Can I request an in-person meeting?

Absolutely. We host visits at our Mysore office and can travel to client locations for kickoff or sensitive discussions.

Is there a minimum project size?

No fixed minimum. We've delivered single-engineer placements and multi-crore site rollouts. We're happy to scope what fits.

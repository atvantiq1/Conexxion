Our staffing practice

Engineers vetted the way engineers vet engineers.

Most staffing firms throw résumés at a job description and hope something sticks. We don't. Our recruiters work alongside senior engineers from our delivery practice — telecom, data center and cloud — so the people we shortlist actually understand the work.

That means a network engineer is interviewed by someone who has lit up RAN sites, a Kubernetes hire is evaluated by someone who has run production clusters, and an SAP Basis profile is screened by a real Basis architect — not a keyword bot.

The result: shorter shortlists, fewer false positives, and engineers who clear your panel on the first or second round, not the fifth.

FAQ

Things clients ask before signing the MSA.

If your question isn't answered here, write to us — we'll respond within one business day with a real human, not a chatbot.

What's your average time-to-shortlist for niche roles?
For roles in our four core practice areas, we typically deliver a 3–5 candidate shortlist within 10–14 working days. Highly niche roles (e.g., specific 5G core skill sets, SAP S/4HANA Basis, OT security) can take 18–25 days.
Do you handle compliance and payroll for contract engineers?
Yes. All contract staff are on Conexxion's payroll with full statutory compliance — PF, ESI, gratuity, professional tax — and are covered by group health insurance. You receive a single consolidated invoice each month.
What's your replacement policy on permanent hires?
We offer a 90-day replacement guarantee on permanent placements at no additional fee — covering both performance issues identified by you and unilateral exits by the candidate.
Can you scale a managed capacity pod up or down quickly?
Yes. Our standard SLA is to ramp up new pod members within 10 working days and ramp down with 30 days' notice. Larger spikes (10+ engineers) require 4–6 weeks of lead time.
Where are your engineers located?
Our active network covers Bangalore, Mysore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Mumbai and the NCR region, with growing depth in Coimbatore, Kochi and Ahmedabad. We support both on-site and remote engagement models.

Need engineers who can actually do the work?

Tell us the role, the timeline, and the deal-breakers. We'll come back with a sourcing plan and a realistic shortlist date — usually inside a business day.

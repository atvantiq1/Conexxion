Our cloud practice

Cloud is easy to start and hard to operate.

Most organizations didn't fail at "moving to cloud." They failed at running it — at the cost shock six months in, the security drift in year two, the platform sprawl in year three. We design cloud estates for the long run, not the proof-of-concept.

That means landing zones with guardrails, FinOps practices that survive past the kickoff, security baselines that are enforced — not posters — and a platform layer that lets your application teams ship instead of filing tickets to ops.

We work across AWS, Azure and hybrid environments — and we're honest about where each shines and where it doesn't.

Migration with cost discipline

Migrations that don't blow up the cloud bill in month four.

Most "lift-and-shift" migrations show their true cost six months in — when over-provisioned VMs, undeleted snapshots and untagged resources have quietly compounded into a 40% overspend. We bake FinOps into the migration itself, not as a year-two project.

  • Right-sized targets validated against utilization data
  • Tagging policies enforced at provisioning
  • Reserved & savings plan modelling pre-cutover
  • Cost dashboards live before the first wave migrates

FAQ

Questions cloud leaders ask before signing the SOW.

If your question isn't here, write to us — we'll respond inside one business day with a real engineer's answer.

Do you have a preferred hyperscaler?
No — we're platform-agnostic. We hold partnerships and certifications across AWS, Azure and have hybrid expertise. We assess the right fit per workload, factoring in your existing skills, commercial relationships and regulatory constraints. If staying on-prem is the right call for a workload, we'll tell you that too.
How do you size a cloud migration commercial?
After a 1–2 week portfolio assessment, we provide a fixed-price proposal for migration foundations and a wave-based price model for the migration itself. We've delivered migrations from 50 workloads to 1,500+ workloads using the same framework.
Can you operate our cloud after the project ends?
Yes. Many clients move into a managed cloud operations engagement after migration — covering 24×7 monitoring, incident response, cost governance, security operations and quarterly architecture reviews. SLAs are scoped per workload tier.
What FinOps tooling do you use?
We're tool-agnostic — we work with native tooling (AWS Cost Explorer, Azure Cost Management), Apptio Cloudability, CloudHealth, Vantage and Kubecost. Our preference is whatever your finance team will actually use; the best tool is the one that doesn't get abandoned in month three.
How do you handle data residency and regulatory compliance?
We design landing zones with data classification baked in, including region-pinning policies, encryption at rest and in transit, key management lifecycles and audit logging that's actually queryable. We've supported clients through SOC 2, ISO 27001 and RBI-related compliance audits.

Cloud strategy, migration or cost recovery?

Tell us where you are and where you'd like to be. We'll come back inside one business day with a realistic next step — usually a free 1-hour assessment call.

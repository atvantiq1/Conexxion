Our DC practice

Engineered like infrastructure. Documented like aviation.

Data center work doesn't reward improvisation. It rewards method. Every Conexxion DC engagement runs on a documented method-of-procedure — every cable label, every PDU port, every cabinet U-position recorded against a master inventory before a single power cord is connected.

That discipline is why our migrations close inside their windows, our cable rooms stay legible five years later, and our operations teams can find a faulty PDU in under three minutes — not three hours.

We work with enterprises building captive data centers, colocation providers, and operators commissioning edge facilities for low-latency workloads.

FAQ

Questions CIOs ask before greenlighting a DC project.

If your question isn't here, write to us — we'll respond inside one business day with a real engineer's answer.

Do you take Tier-III certification through Uptime Institute?
We design and build to Tier-III aligned standards and have led clients through Uptime Institute design and constructed-facility certifications. Whether formal certification is pursued is a client commercial decision — we'll be transparent about cost and timeline impact upfront.
What's your typical migration window planning approach?
We typically run a 4–6 week planning phase that includes inventory verification, application-dependency mapping, freeze-window definition, cutover wave planning and at least one tabletop dry run. Migration execution is then planned across one or more change windows with documented rollback triggers.
Can you support multi-vendor environments (HPE, Dell, Cisco, etc.)?
Yes. Our DC engineers are certified across major OEMs including HPE, Dell EMC, Cisco UCS, Lenovo, Pure, NetApp, IBM and Huawei. We typically map specific OEM coverage at the kickoff against your installed base.
What's included in your managed operations SLA?
A standard managed operations engagement covers 24×7 onsite presence, 30-minute response on P1 alarms, environmental and capacity monitoring via DCIM, monthly capacity and incident reports, quarterly governance, and spare-parts logistics. SLA tiers are customized per facility criticality.
Do you handle cabling certification and as-built documentation?
Yes — every Conexxion build is handed over with complete as-built documentation: rack elevations, cable schedules, port-to-port maps, power schematics, BMS diagrams and certified cabling test results (Fluke or equivalent). It's the boring deliverable that pays for itself in year three.

Our telecom practice

From survey to SLA — one accountable team.

Telecom rollouts fail in the seams — between RF and civil, between civil and electrical, between handover and operations. We've spent ten years closing those seams. Our crews are organized as integrated pods that own the entire site lifecycle: survey, design, civil, installation, integration, optimization and handover.

That means one project manager, one weekly tracker, one accountable signature on the acceptance certificate — and far fewer of the "the other vendor was supposed to do that" conversations that turn 90-day rollouts into 9-month rollouts.

We work with Tier-1 operators, regional ISPs, infrastructure providers and OEM system integrators on engagements ranging from 50 to 5,000 sites.

How we work

Field execution that respects engineering reality.

Telecom is field work. Software discipline meets civil discipline meets crew safety. Here's how we run our practice.

5G Rollout · Tier-1 Operator

5G greenfield deployment with crew-level accountability.

Each site is owned by a five-person pod — RF lead, civil lead, electrical lead, integrator and a quality auditor — with a daily stand-up against a shared tracker. No site is closed until all five sign off, and every defect carries the name of the engineer responsible for fix.

  • OEM-certified crews on 5G NSA & SA
  • Daily KPIs published to client dashboards
  • Quality auditor independent of execution chain
  • Site-acceptance certificate signed inside 48 hours of integration
Fiber / FTTx

Last-mile fiber crews trained for tier-2 and tier-3 cities.

Our FTTx crews specialize in the unglamorous, hard-to-execute work that defines real broadband coverage outside metros — narrow lanes, mixed permissions, legacy ducts and unpredictable ground conditions. We bring the local knowledge and the OFC discipline together.

  • OFC route survey with permission-readiness scoring
  • HDD & open trenching crews on call
  • OTDR-tested splicing with traceable lot numbers
  • Standard 14-day site-to-site delivery, faster on plan

24×7 NOC and field-operations as a single accountable layer.

Once your network is live, we run it. Our managed services pair a tiered NOC with dispatched field engineers across India — with a clear escalation matrix, SLA scorecard and quarterly business reviews chaired by our director-level practice leads.

  • Multi-vendor NOC capability across major OEMs

FAQ

Questions operators ask before signing the SOW.

If your question isn't here, write to us — we'll respond inside one business day with a real engineer's answer.

Which OEM ecosystems do your crews support?
Our engineers are certified across major OEMs including Nokia, Ericsson, Samsung and ZTE for RAN, plus Cisco, Juniper and Huawei for transport. Specific OEM coverage is mapped at the kickoff call to align with your existing vendor stack.
Can you handle EPC-style turnkey rollouts?
Yes. We deliver full turnkey scopes including civil, electrical, tower works, equipment installation, integration and acceptance — with a single project manager and a single commercial owner. Most of our 100+ site engagements are turnkey.
What's your typical site-acceptance timeline?
For a standard greenfield 4G/5G macro site with all permissions in place, we deliver from kickoff to integration in 14–18 working days. Brownfield upgrades typically run 5–8 working days. Fiber rollouts are quoted per kilometre and per route complexity.
How do you handle safety and compliance on tower works?
All tower crews carry valid working-at-height certifications, climber-safety harnesses, and rescue training. Each site has a designated safety officer, daily toolbox talks, and a documented JSA. We have completed 1,200+ sites with zero lost-time injuries.
Can you take over a stalled rollout from another vendor?
Yes — and we frequently do. Our intake process for distressed rollouts includes an audit of completed work, a defect-and-rework register, and a re-baselined delivery plan inside two weeks of takeover.

Have a rollout that needs to happen?

Whether it's 50 sites or 5,000, we'll come back inside one business day with a realistic delivery plan and a named project lead.
